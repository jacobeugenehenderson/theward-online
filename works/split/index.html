The rules

Menu prices match in-store

What a restaurant charges here is what it charges at the counter. The comparison above only holds because of it — the incumbents’ menus are marked up to survive their commission, and there is little reason to do it here.

Tips are the courier's, entire

Not split, not matched against a wage floor, not counted toward anyone else's share.

Processing is a pass-through

"charged to the customer and used solely to cover processing costs." What is charged must be what is paid.

The Local Host is paid from the platform's own share

A division of what the platform already collects. Nobody pays more because a Local Host exists.
