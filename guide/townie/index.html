Townie

A Townie is someone who spends time in the neighborhood. It’s the Ward’s way of recognizing that you’re part of its everyday life.

Becoming a Townie

Check in by scanning a code posted at a Place.

Check in on three different days within two weeks. Two check-ins on the same day still count as one day.

If you verify that you live here or claim a Place you look after, you become a Townie immediately.

What it opens

Townies can post and reply on Bulletin, rate and review places, and start private messages.

Everything else stays open. You never need to become a Townie just to look around, search, read Bulletin or see what’s happening.
